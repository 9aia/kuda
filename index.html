kuda is the org's code-map engine — it turns a checkout into verified per-module cards that agents load before planning instead of re-exploring from scratch. It owns repo memory (extraction, cards, the symbol graph, freshness) and nothing else: verification is mimus's job, distribution is tamias's, orchestration is peregrin's.
